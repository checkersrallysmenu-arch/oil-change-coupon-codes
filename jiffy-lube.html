Common questions

Does Jiffy Lube always have a coupon? Almost always. The $10 off offer runs widely, and most regions have a $30 off or percentage deal in any given month. The exact offer depends on your store's franchise group.

Can coupons be combined? No. One coupon per visit is the standard rule, so pick the largest discount your store accepts.

Do the coupons work on synthetic oil? Most dollar-off coupons apply to any oil change including full synthetic, but read the terms on the coupon itself, because a few regional offers name a specific service.
