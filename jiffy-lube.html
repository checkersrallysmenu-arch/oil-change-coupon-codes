Why does Jiffy Lube have different coupons in different states?

Jiffy Lube has no single national coupon list. Franchise groups publish their own coupons, so a store in Florida and a store in Oregon can run completely different deals on the same day. How to use a Jiffy Lube coupon, step by step

  1. Open the official coupon page and enter your zip code, so you see your own store's group offers, not another region's.
  2. Match the code to your store before you drive out. A code from another group's page will not be honored.
  3. Show the coupon on your phone at check in, before the service starts.
  4. Check the invoice shows the discount as its own line, and read the coupon terms for oil type and quart limits.
