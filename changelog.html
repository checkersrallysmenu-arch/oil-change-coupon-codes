How to read this log

An entry is written whenever offers are re-checked and changed, a new page or property launches, or a claim is tested and corrected. Quiet weeks with no changes get no entry, we would rather show an honest gap than a padded one. The dataset itself is at data.html, and the checking process is documented on the methodology page.
