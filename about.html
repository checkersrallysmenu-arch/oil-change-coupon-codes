About This Site

This is an independent coupon dataset, not a brand site and not a coupon aggregator. It exists because oil change coupons are hard to trust: aggregator lists copy each other, keep expired codes, and invent promo codes for chains that have no promo code system at all.

Every offer here traces to an official source, the brand's own coupon page or the franchise group page that runs the stores, and every row carries the date it was last checked. The full method is on the methodology page, and every change is recorded on the update history page. The dataset itself is free to reuse under CC BY 4.0, with its dates and sources kept attached.

What this site is not

  • Not affiliated with Jiffy Lube, Valvoline, Firestone or Take 5. Brand names belong to their owners.
  • Not a booking site. Coupons are claimed on the brands' own pages and shown at the store.
  • Not a guarantee. Offers vary by store and can end without notice, so confirm with your store before you go.

Corrections

If you find an offer here that a store will not honor, or a date that has moved, the fastest route is the contact page on the main listings site, where the full store by store listings live. Corrections are verified against the official page and applied in the next cycle. The method page explains the recheck cycles: Jiffy Lube $30 off every day, Jiffy Lube hub every 3 days, Valvoline campaigns every week, Firestone and Take 5 every week.
