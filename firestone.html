Common questions

Is the $19.99 oil change still real? Yes, it continues at participating stores, usually for a conventional oil change with a standard filter. Not every store runs it at the same time.

Which Firestone oil deal saves the most? If you run full synthetic, the Pennzoil and Rotella offers can beat the flat $15 off. For a basic conventional change, the $15 off or the $19.99 special wins.

Do Firestone offers stack? Generally no. Instant discounts and prepaid card offers have their own terms, so read the fine print on the offers page.
