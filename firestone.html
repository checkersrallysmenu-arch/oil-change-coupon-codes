The $19.99 special, honestly

Many coupon sites still list a Firestone $19.99 oil change coupon as if it were live everywhere. It is not. The $19.99 coupon itself expired years ago, and the wording traces to a 2017 TV spot. Some participating stores still advertise a $19.99 special locally, usually for a conventional oil change with a standard filter, but participation varies and no national $19.99 coupon should be assumed. Check your local store's page first, and use the $15 off offer as the deal you can actually claim nationally.

Firestone runs no promo code system for these oil offers and no regional variation was found in the verified offers. If a site lists a Firestone promo code, treat it as unverified until it appears on the official offers page.
