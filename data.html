Every row is a coupon or offer that was live on an official brand or franchise page on the date in its last_checked column. Expired offers are removed at each update instead of being left to rot, which is what makes this file different from the coupon lists scraped onto aggregator sites.

What the columns mean

ColumnMeaning
brandThe chain running the offer.
offerWhat the coupon takes off, and which service it applies to.
code_or_claimThe printed code, or how the coupon is claimed when there is no code (text or email signup, ID at the shop, and so on).
expiry_dateThe date printed on the official offer. Rolling campaigns are marked Ongoing and are re-checked on the cycle below.
scopeWhere the offer works: national, a region, or one franchise group's stores.
official_sourceThe official page the offer was verified on.
last_checkedThe last date a human verified this row against the official source.

How to cite this dataset

Oil Change Coupons Dataset, version 1.2 (October 31, 2026), maintained by Oil Change Coupon Codes. The live lists behind this dataset, including store by store Jiffy Lube coupon sets, live at Oil Change Coupon Codes. If you republish an offer from this dataset, keep its last checked date and its official source with it. An offer stripped of its date is how stale lists are born. This dataset is shared under CC BY 4.0.

What this dataset will never do

  • Relabel an old check date as a new one to look fresh.
  • Keep an expired offer in the file with a quietly extended expiry. Rolling campaign dates only move when the official page moves them, and they are labelled Ongoing in between checks.
  • Call an aggregator code verified. Every row traces back to an official brand or franchise page.
  • List an offer with no check date at all.

The rules behind these promises are written out on the methodology page, and every change to the dataset is recorded on the changelog page.
